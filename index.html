B2B Core Pickup Request & ERP Lifecycle Integration
Architecture & Prototype

An end-to-end reverse logistics architecture designed to eliminate paper manifests and phone calls by digitizing lead-acid battery core collections within the enterprise portal.

B2B DoorDash Hot-Shot Dispatch & Multi-Location Mapping
Technical Specification

A localized 45-minute dispatch engine designed to capture emergency wholesale orders and counter scheduled weekly competitor routes.

Pre-Built POS & Shop Management Connectors (cXML/Webhooks)
Strategy & Architecture

A strategic initiative to embed our B2B platform directly into mid-market dealer operations (QuickBooks, Lightspeed, CDK Global) using lightweight APIs and cXML PunchOut, maximizing technical switching costs.

  • Unified Source of Truth: Routes automated orders through Magento to ensure dealer visibility on the portal dashboard, avoiding "silent" direct-to-ERP EDI bypasses.
  • Fleet e-Procurement: Unlocks cXML PunchOut capabilities required by enterprise fleets using software like SAP Ariba and Coupa.
  • Centralized Business Logic: Validates complex commercial rules (MSI stock allocation, volume pricing) at the portal layer before hitting NetSuite.

NetSuite Automated Delivery Route & Schedule Architecture
Data Governance & ERP

A systems-level ERP overhaul abstracting a multi-departmental data entry problem into a single automated NetSuite rule, achieving 100% data integrity for portal frontend features.

  • Root-Cause Abstraction: Pivoted from building a flawed UI to fixing upstream ERP data mapping between Servicing Routes and Delivery Days.
  • Cross-Functional Leverage: Eliminated redundant manual entry for branch operations while unlocking dynamic 48-hour order cutoff rules for e-commerce.
  • Execution Roadmap: Orchestrated NetSuite schema updates, historical data backfills, and subsequent Magento API integrations.
