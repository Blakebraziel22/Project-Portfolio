B2B DoorDash Hot-Shot Dispatch & Multi-Location Mapping
Validated Spec • Roadmap Backlog
Modeled Opportunity: $1.5M+ uncaptured emergency market TAM & ~35% projected commercial churn reduction

A localized 45-minute dispatch engine designed to capture emergency wholesale orders and counter scheduled weekly competitor routes.

  • Address-Entity Branch Mapping: Extends Magento's address schema with assigned_branch_source_code, allowing enterprise buyers to toggle between distinct child locations and servicing branches in the header.
  • Tiered Weight & Courier Dispatch Matrix: Calibrated delivery logistics across Standard (0–100 lbs @ $20 sedan) and Heavy Express (101–300 lbs @ $40 pickup/van) tiers, operating on a 100% margin-retention flat pass-through model.
  • Zero NetSuite API Overhead: Leverages Adobe Commerce Multi-Source Inventory (MSI) to evaluate local branch inventory client-side on PLPs and search pages, preventing ERP concurrency rate limits.
Architecture Note: No custom UI prototype was required. The front-end framework already exists on our B2C platform ("My Store" selector); this specification extends the address entity and repurposes production UI assets for complex B2B multi-location hierarchies.
